How to use the calculator

Supported sizes: 110g, 227g, and 450g threaded isobutane canisters. Gross weight = empty can weight + remaining fuel.

  • Type the gross weight (in grams) of each can by size. Add as many rows as you have cans.
  • The solver finds an optimal transfer plan that reduces total cans carried and empty weight.
  • See a donor/recipient graph, exact gram transfers, and total carry weight once inputs are valid.

Tip: Values lighter than an empty can or heavier than its capacity are flagged so you can correct typos quickly.

        Frequently asked questions

        How much fuel is left in my isobutane canister?

        Weigh the canister on a kitchen scale and subtract the empty can weight: about 101 g for a 110 g can, 147 g for a 227 g can, and 216 g for a 450 g can. The remainder is fuel in grams.

        Can you transfer fuel between isobutane canisters?

        Yes. A threaded canister-to-canister transfer adapter connects two Lindal-valve cans. Chill the receiving can and warm the donor can so pressure moves fuel downhill, then verify the result on a scale. Do it outdoors, away from flames, and never fill a can above its rated capacity.

        Does this calculator work with Jetboil, Snow Peak, GSI, or Coleman canisters?

        Yes. Most 110 g, 230 g, and 450 g isobutane canisters share the same threaded Lindal valve and very similar empty weights, so the MSR figures are a close match. If your brand's empty weight differs by a few grams, the plan is still correct; only the reported remaining fuel shifts slightly.

        What does the calculator optimize?

        It finds the set of cans with the lowest total empty weight that can hold all of your fuel, then the fewest transfers and fewest grams moved to fill them. Everything runs in your browser; no data is uploaded. Read how the solver works for the details.
